About

I am a full-stack web developer who builds responsive SaaS applications, operational dashboards, business websites, and database-backed workflows using Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Playwright, and Git/GitHub, deployed on Vercel and Netlify. I am currently developing Event Core, a multi-module hall and event management SaaS platform for a New York-based client, covering bookings, calendar availability, kitchen operations, dining and seating, hall layouts, billing, reports, vendors, staff roles, and permissions. I have also worked on the Lux Kitchens website and its inquiry automation workflows.

I use ChatGPT, OpenAI Codex, Claude, and Base44 to accelerate prototyping, debugging, refactoring, and test generation, while personally reviewing and validating every piece of production code. I translate client conversations and real venue-operation requirements into implementation plans, UI behavior, and production-ready features. My background in IT support and image/data annotation adds strong troubleshooting, documentation, quality-control, and client-communication skills, so I approach development from the user's side and focus on shipping reliable work that can be improved over time.
